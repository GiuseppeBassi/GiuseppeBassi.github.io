## About this site  

I am Giuseppe Bassi, a PhD student in Operations Research and Financial Engineering at Princeton University. I graduated with a BSc in Mathematical and Computing Sciences for Artificial Intelligence from Bocconi University. I am interested in high-dimensional probability, optimization, asymptotic analysis, and the mathematical foundations of machine learning, particularly through the interplay of concentration, convex duality, and statistical physics.

This website collects my university notes and some materials from Mathematical Olympiads, with more to come as the content grows.  

If you notice mistakes or have suggestions for improvement feel free to contact me at [giuseppe.bassi@princeton.edu](mailto:giuseppe.bassi@princeton.edu).
